Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 June 2025 — Jurisdiction: England and Wales

What we collect

When you submit a schedule request, we store the data you enter (team names, venues, time slots, blackout dates) on our servers to generate and deliver your fixture list. We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal information unless you contact us directly.

We do not require you to create an account. There are no accounts on this service.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your payment card number. Only your job reference is passed to Stripe so we can confirm your payment. Stripe's full privacy policy is at stripe.com/gb/privacy.

Data retention

Your schedule job (the input constraints and generated fixture list) is automatically deleted 30 days after it was created. No manual action is required on your part.

Cookies

This site does not use tracking or advertising cookies. Stripe may set cookies during the payment flow; those are governed by Stripe's own policy.

Third-party services

  • Stripe — payment processing only
  • Hosting provider — server logs (IP address, request path, timestamp) are retained for up to 14 days for security and debugging purposes

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties beyond the payment processor described above.

Your rights (UK GDPR)

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, or erase personal data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, email privacy@schedule-it.com.

Contact

Schedule-it is operated as a sole trader in the United Kingdom.
Email: privacy@schedule-it.com
