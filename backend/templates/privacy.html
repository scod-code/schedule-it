Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 June 2025

What we collect

When you submit a schedule request, we store the data you enter (team names, venues, time slots) on our servers so we can generate your fixture list and deliver it to you after payment. We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal information unless you contact us directly.

When you pay, Stripe processes your card details. We never see or store your payment card number. Stripe's privacy policy is available at stripe.com/gb/privacy.

How long we keep your data

Your schedule job (the input constraints and generated fixture list) is automatically deleted 30 days after it was created. No manual action is required.

Cookies

This site does not use tracking or advertising cookies. Stripe may set cookies during the payment flow; those are governed by Stripe's policy.

Third-party services

  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Your hosting provider — server logs (IP address, request path, timestamp) are retained for up to 14 days for security and debugging purposes.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, or erase personal data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, email us at privacy@schedule-it.app.

Contact

Schedule-it is operated as a sole trader in the United Kingdom.
Email: privacy@schedule-it.app
