Refund Policy

Last updated: 1 June 2025

Our promise

We only ask for payment after a mathematically valid, clash-free schedule has been generated. If you are not satisfied with your schedule for any reason, email us within 14 days of purchase and we will issue a full refund — no questions asked.

How to request a refund

  1. Email refunds@schedule-it.app with the subject "Refund request".
  2. Include your job ID (visible on the download page and in your Stripe receipt).
  3. We will process the refund within 5 business days. Stripe typically returns funds in 5–10 business days after that.

Exceptions

We cannot issue refunds more than 30 days after the original purchase, or for jobs where the download link has been accessed more than three times (indicating the schedule has been used). In practice we apply common sense — if something went wrong, contact us.
