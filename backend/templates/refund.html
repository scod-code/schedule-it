Refund Policy

Last updated: 1 June 2025

Our commitment

We only ask for payment after a mathematically valid, clash-free schedule has been generated. If schedule generation fails, or if the schedule contains demonstrable errors (double-bookings, violated blackout dates), you are entitled to a full refund.

When refunds are available

  • The schedule generation failed or returned an error after payment
  • The schedule contains a verifiable constraint violation (e.g. a team double-booked)
  • You have not yet downloaded the completed schedule
  • Your request is made within 14 days of purchase

When refunds are not available

Once a correct schedule has been downloaded, we cannot issue refunds — the deliverable has been provided. We also cannot refund requests made more than 30 days after the original purchase.

How to request a refund

  1. Email refunds@schedule-it.com with subject "Refund request".
  2. Include your job reference (visible on the download page and in your Stripe receipt).
  3. Briefly describe the issue.
  4. We will respond within 5 business days and process any approved refund promptly. Stripe typically returns funds within 5–10 business days after that.

Contact
